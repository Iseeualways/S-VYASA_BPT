About the university

S-VYASA Deemed-to-be University Prashanti Kutiram Campus, Bangalore

S-VYASA is a NAAC A+ accredited Deemed-to-be University, founded by Padma Shri awardee Dr. H. R. Nagendra. Its Bachelor of Physiotherapy is offered through the School of Physiotherapy at the Prashanti Kutiram Campus in Jigani, Anekal, Bangalore.

The programme runs for five years: four years of academic study followed by a compulsory one-year rotatory clinical internship. Students learn to assess, diagnose and manage movement and functional impairments caused by injury, illness, disability, ageing and lifestyle-related conditions, with training in therapeutic exercise, electrotherapy, functional rehabilitation, pain management and preventive healthcare, and clinical rotations at Cadabam’s Hospitals.

02 — Eligibility

Who can apply for BPT

Below is a list of details that makes one eligible for S-VYASA University BPT Admission.

50%

General Criteria

  • Passed 10+2 or an equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry and Biology (or Botany and Zoology).
  • A minimum aggregate of 50% in Physics, Chemistry and Biology taken together.
  • English passed as a subject, mandatory for all categories.
40%

Criteria for Reserved Categories

  • SC, ST and OBC-NCL candidates: minimum aggregate of 40% in Physics, Chemistry and Biology taken together.
  • Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD/PwD): minimum aggregate of 40% in the same subjects.

09 — FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is the BPT programme at S-VYASA University?

The Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) is a five-year, full-time programme taught in English through the School of Physiotherapy. The S-VYASA BPT course combines four years of academic study with a compulsory one-year rotatory clinical internship.

Where is the BPT programme offered?

The programme is taught at the Prashanti Kutiram Campus in Jigani, Anekal, Bangalore. Students can check up more details for BPT admission in Bangalore 2027.

What is the eligibility for BPT at S-VYASA?

Candidates must have passed 10+2 or an equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry and Biology (or Botany and Zoology), with a minimum aggregate of 50% in these subjects taken together in order to qualify for S-VYASA BPT eligibility. The minimum is 40% for SC, ST and OBC-NCL candidates and for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities. A pass in English is mandatory for all categories.

What will I study in the BPT programme?

In the S-VYASA Bachelor of Physiotherapy course, you will learn to assess, diagnose and manage movement and functional impairments caused by injury, illness, disability, ageing and lifestyle-related conditions. Training covers therapeutic exercise, electrotherapy, functional rehabilitation, pain management and preventive healthcare.

Does the BPT programme include clinical training?

Yes, students complete clinical rotations at Cadabam’s Hospitals. The programme ends with a compulsory one-year rotatory internship, which begins after all academic examinations are completed.
