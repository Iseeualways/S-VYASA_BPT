S-VYASA University (Deemed-to-be University), Prashanti Kutiram Campus, Bengaluru

S-VYASA is a NAAC A+ accredited Deemed-to-be University committed to excellence in higher education, research and holistic development. Founded by Padma Shri awardee Dr. H. R. Nagendra, it offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across engineering, computer applications, management, allied health and yoga.

The BPT is offered through the School of Physiotherapy at the Prashanti Kutiram Campus in Jigani, Anekal. Students learn to assess, diagnose and manage movement and functional impairments caused by injury, illness, disability, ageing and lifestyle-related conditions, with training in therapeutic exercise, electrotherapy, functional rehabilitation, pain management and preventive healthcare.

02 — Eligibility

Who can apply for BPT

Eligibility follows the official S-VYASA criteria for the Bachelor of Physiotherapy.

50%

General criteria

  • Passed 10+2 or an equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry and Biology (or Botany and Zoology).
  • A minimum aggregate of 50% in Physics, Chemistry and Biology taken together.
  • English passed as a subject, mandatory for all categories.
40%

Relaxed criteria

  • SC, ST and OBC-NCL candidates: minimum aggregate of 40% in Physics, Chemistry and Biology taken together.
  • Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD/PwD): minimum aggregate of 40% in the same subjects.
  • English remains mandatory for all categories.

09 — FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is the BPT programme at S-VYASA University?

The Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) is a 5-year full-time undergraduate programme: four years of academic study followed by a compulsory one-year rotatory clinical internship. It prepares students to assess, diagnose and manage movement and functional impairments through evidence-based physiotherapy.

Where is the BPT programme offered?

Through the School of Physiotherapy at the Prashanti Kutiram Campus, Vivekananda Road, Kalluballu Post, Jigani, Anekal, Bengaluru.

What is the eligibility for BPT at S-VYASA?

Candidates must have passed 10+2 or an equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry and Biology (or Botany and Zoology), with at least 50% aggregate in these subjects, and must have passed English. For SC, ST, OBC-NCL and PwBD/PwD candidates, the minimum aggregate is 40%. English is mandatory for all categories.

What will I study in the BPT programme?

Anatomy, physiology, biomechanics, exercise therapy, electrotherapy and movement sciences, followed by musculoskeletal, neurological, cardiopulmonary, paediatric, sports and community physiotherapy.

Does the BPT programme include clinical training?

Yes. Students gain hands-on experience through practical sessions, case discussions and clinical rotations, including rotations at Cadabam’s Hospitals under experienced physiotherapists. A compulsory one-year rotatory internship follows the academic years.

What is the BPT fee at S-VYASA University?

For Indian students in 2026-27, tuition is ₹1,00,000 per year for four years (₹4,00,000 in total). The total excluding hostel is ₹4,62,000. Hostel costs ₹85,000 per year, and the total including hostel for all five years is ₹8,87,000.

What skills will I develop during the BPT programme?

Physiotherapy assessment, therapeutic exercise, electrotherapy, functional rehabilitation, pain management, patient education, clinical documentation and evidence-based decision-making, along with communication, teamwork, ethical practice and professional accountability.

What career opportunities are available after BPT?

Hospitals, physiotherapy clinics, rehabilitation centres, sports organisations, community healthcare, geriatric care, women’s health, schools, corporate health, fitness and wellness centres, and academic and research institutions.

Can I pursue higher studies after completing BPT?

Yes. Graduates can pursue postgraduate education such as the Master of Physiotherapy (MPT) and explore specialised clinical, academic, research and professional pathways.

Is S-VYASA University a private or government institution?

S-VYASA is a private Deemed-to-be University, recognised by the UGC and accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC.
